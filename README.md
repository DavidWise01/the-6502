# THE MOS 6502

> A sphere of **UD0** — the ROOT0 universe / biosphere. Domain: **EXEREÚNESIS**.

THE MOS 6502 (6502) — a UD0 EXEREÚNESIS teardown + spec edition. The 6502 is the most consequential cheap chip in computing history. At about a quarter the price of its rivals, this tiny 8-bit processor powered the Apple II, the Commodore 64, the Atari machines, the BBC Micro — and, in derived form, the NES. It put computers and consoles in millions of homes. Render-not-invent, two-layer (datasheet fact vs lore); no ROMs/BIOS/code; manufacturer cited not minted.
